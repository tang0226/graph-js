Node image downscaling test

Every node shows a 1-pixel black-and-white checkerboard. Shrunk properly, it's a uniform mid-grey. Black or white patches, speckle, or moiré rings mean the browser's resize is skipping pixels (aliasing). Zoom with the wheel or the buttons, drag to pan. Magenta means the image isn't drawn at all. Zoom in far enough and the checkerboard itself shows: that's expected once a node is larger than the source.
